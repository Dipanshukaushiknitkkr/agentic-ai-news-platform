# Runway launches AI model router as generative media gets crowded

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 17:07:35 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/runway-bets-on-ai-model-routing-as-generative-media-gets-crowded/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/runway-bets-on-ai-model-routing-as-generative-media-gets-crowded/)

**LLM Summary:** Runway, a company focused on generative media, has introduced the Media Router, an AI tool that streamlines the process of choosing the right model for generating images, videos, or audio. This tool simplifies the decision-making process by automatically selecting the best model based on a developer's priorities, whether it's quality, speed, or cost. This can help developers work more efficiently and produce high-quality content.

The Media Router is a tool that automatically selects the best image, video, or audio generation model for a request based on whether a developer prioritizes quality, speed or cost.
