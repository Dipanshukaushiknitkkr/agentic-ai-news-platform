# Bluesky’s AI assistant Attie expands into an open social research tool

**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 15:13:57 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/blueskys-ai-assistant-attie-expands-into-an-open-social-research-tool/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/blueskys-ai-assistant-attie-expands-into-an-open-social-research-tool/)

**LLM Summary:** Bluesky's AI assistant, Attie, has been expanded to serve as an open social research tool. This means users can now ask Attie questions about current news, trends, and conversations happening on Bluesky and other apps connected to the AT Protocol. Attie's new capabilities enable users to explore and analyze social data in a more interactive and accessible way.
