# Tesla’s robotaxis are moving in reverse

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 16:29:59 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/teslas-robotaxis-are-moving-in-reverse/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/teslas-robotaxis-are-moving-in-reverse/)

**LLM Summary:** Tesla's robotaxi service has seen a significant decline in the number of paid miles traveled, dropping 36% in the second quarter. This decline occurred despite the expansion of the service to new cities. The drop in robotaxi usage could be a concern for Tesla's autonomous driving ambitions and future revenue streams.

The number of paid robotaxi miles traveled fell 36% in the second quarter, despite expanding to new cities, according to Tesla's own figures.
