# OpenAI makes ChatGPT Health available to all US users

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/openai-makes-chatgpt-health-available-to-all-u-s-users/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/openai-makes-chatgpt-health-available-to-all-u-s-users/)

**LLM Summary:** OpenAI has expanded its ChatGPT Health feature to all US users, allowing them to access personalized health insights. Additionally, users can now link their health data from popular services like Apple Health, Function, and MyFitnessPal to gain a more comprehensive view of their well-being. This integration aims to provide users with a more holistic understanding of their health.

Users can also integrate their personal data from services like Apple Health, Function, and MyFitnessPal.
