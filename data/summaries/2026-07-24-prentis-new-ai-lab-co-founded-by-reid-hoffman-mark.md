# Prentis, new AI lab co-founded by Reid Hoffman, Mark Pincus in talks to raise $100M

**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 22:25:58 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/prentis-new-ai-lab-co-founded-by-reid-hoffman-mark-pincus-in-talks-to-raise-100m/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/prentis-new-ai-lab-co-founded-by-reid-hoffman-mark-pincus-in-talks-to-raise-100m/)

**LLM Summary:** Prentis, a new AI lab co-founded by Reid Hoffman and Mark Pincus, is reportedly in talks to secure a $100 million investment. The lab is focused on developing AI that can automate routine computer tasks, which they believe will become a more significant use case for AI than coding in the near future. This automation could revolutionize industries by freeing up human resources for more complex and creative tasks.

The neolab is betting that automating routine computer tasks will soon outpace coding as AI's biggest use case.
