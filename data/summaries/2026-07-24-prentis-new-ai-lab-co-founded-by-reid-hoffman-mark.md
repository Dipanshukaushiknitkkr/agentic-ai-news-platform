# Prentis, new AI lab co-founded by Reid Hoffman, Mark Pincus in talks to raise $100M

**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 22:25:58 +0000

**LLM Summary:** Prentis, a new AI lab co-founded by tech industry veterans Reid Hoffman and Mark Pincus, is reportedly in talks to secure $100 million in funding. The lab believes that automating routine computer tasks will become AI's primary use case, surpassing traditional coding. This shift in focus could lead to significant advancements in AI technology and its practical applications.

The neolab is betting that automating routine computer tasks will soon outpace coding as AI's biggest use case.
