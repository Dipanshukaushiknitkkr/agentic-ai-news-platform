# India’s move against Jack Dorsey’s Bitchat sparks legal debate

**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 16:54:58 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/indias-move-against-jack-dorseys-bitchat-sparks-legal-debate/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/indias-move-against-jack-dorseys-bitchat-sparks-legal-debate/)

**LLM Summary:** India is cracking down on Bitchat, a popular offline messaging app created by Jack Dorsey, which has seen a surge in popularity amidst protests in New Delhi. The move has sparked a legal debate, with the app's growing user base raising questions about its legitimacy and compliance with Indian laws. The decision to target Bitchat highlights the complexities of regulating messaging apps in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.

The offline messaging app surged in popularity in India amid protests in New Delhi.
