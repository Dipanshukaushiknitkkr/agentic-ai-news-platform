# Tesla’s door handles may spur new US safety rules

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 20:06:21 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/teslas-door-handles-may-spur-new-u-s-safety-rules/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/teslas-door-handles-may-spur-new-u-s-safety-rules/)

**LLM Summary:** The US government is considering new safety regulations after a series of incidents, including fatalities, where people became trapped inside Tesla vehicles due to the electric car manufacturer's unique door handles. These incidents have raised concerns about the safety and accessibility of Tesla's design. The proposed rule-making process aims to address these issues and ensure the safety of drivers and passengers.

The new rule-making process follows a series of incidents, including fatal ones, in which people have become stuck inside cars.
