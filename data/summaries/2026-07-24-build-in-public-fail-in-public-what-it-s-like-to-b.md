# Build in public, fail in public: what it’s like to be a founder under 20 right now

**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/build-in-public-fail-in-public-what-its-like-to-be-a-founder-under-20-right-now/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/build-in-public-fail-in-public-what-its-like-to-be-a-founder-under-20-right-now/)

**LLM Summary:** The rise of AI tools has democratized the startup process, allowing young founders under 20 to launch successful companies quickly. This shift has shortened the timelines to success and enabled more young entrepreneurs to build innovative businesses without the need for traditional corporate experience. As a result, being a young founder today means being open to building and failing in public.

AI tools have democratized the opportunity to build, shortening the timelines of success and enabling more young people to start successful companies without stepping foot inside a Big Tech company.
