# Waymo reportedly mulling a breakup with Uber

**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 20:43:35 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/waymo-reportedly-mulling-a-breakup-with-uber/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/waymo-reportedly-mulling-a-breakup-with-uber/)

**LLM Summary:** Waymo and Uber have a partnership contract set to expire in May 2028. According to reports, Waymo may be considering ending this partnership. The two companies have been working together on autonomous driving technology, but the partnership's future remains uncertain.

The contract between the two companies ends in May 2028, Uber told TechCrunch.
