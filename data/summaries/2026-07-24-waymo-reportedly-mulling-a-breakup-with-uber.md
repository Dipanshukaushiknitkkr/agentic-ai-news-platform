# Waymo reportedly mulling a breakup with Uber

**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 20:43:35 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/waymo-reportedly-mulling-a-breakup-with-uber/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/waymo-reportedly-mulling-a-breakup-with-uber/)

**LLM Summary:** Waymo, a self-driving car company, is reportedly considering ending its partnership with Uber. The contract between the two companies is set to expire in May 2028, and it appears that Waymo may not renew it. This potential breakup could have significant implications for the future of autonomous vehicles and the companies involved.

The contract between the two companies ends in May 2028, Uber told TechCrunch.
