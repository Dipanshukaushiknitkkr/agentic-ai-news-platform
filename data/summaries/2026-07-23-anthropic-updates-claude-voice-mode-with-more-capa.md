# Anthropic updates Claude voice mode with more capable models

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/anthropic-updates-claude-voice-mode-with-more-capable-models/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/anthropic-updates-claude-voice-mode-with-more-capable-models/)

**LLM Summary:** Anthropic has updated its Claude voice model, allowing for more advanced capabilities such as rescheduling meetings and drafting emails. This upgrade aims to make the AI more useful in real-world scenarios, enabling users to perform tasks with greater ease and efficiency. The updated model is a step towards making AI-powered virtual assistants more practical and user-friendly.

Claude's new voice model will let you reschedule your meeting or draft an email.
