# Patreon lays off 20% of its workforce

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 20:30:07 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/patreon-lays-off-off-20-of-its-workforce/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/patreon-lays-off-off-20-of-its-workforce/)

**LLM Summary:** Patreon, a popular platform for creators to earn money from their fans, has laid off 20% of its workforce. The company's CEO, Conte, stated that the core business remains strong but that adjustments are needed to respond to changing market conditions and ensure stability. This cost-cutting measure aims to help Patreon stay competitive in a rapidly evolving tech landscape.

In a memo to staff that was posted online, Conte said the company's core business is strong, but the platform has to respond to market changes and adjust its cost structure to remain stable.
