# US government says Iran-linked hackers are disrupting American water and energy providers

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 17:27:08 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/us-government-says-iran-linked-hackers-are-disrupting-american-water-and-energy-providers/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/us-government-says-iran-linked-hackers-are-disrupting-american-water-and-energy-providers/)

**LLM Summary:** US government officials have issued a warning that Iranian hackers are targeting the systems of American water and energy providers. These hackers are exploiting vulnerabilities in the systems to disrupt operations, posing a potential threat to public safety and infrastructure. The advisory serves as a call to action for these providers to strengthen their cybersecurity measures to prevent such attacks.

An updated government advisory warns that Iranian hackers are exploiting systems used by water and energy providers.
