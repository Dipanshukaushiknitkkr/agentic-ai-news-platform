# Meta drops out of a major clean energy pact as its natural gas buildout accelerates

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 19:41:54 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/meta-drops-out-of-a-major-clean-energy-pact-as-its-natural-gas-buildout-accelerates/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/meta-drops-out-of-a-major-clean-energy-pact-as-its-natural-gas-buildout-accelerates/)

**LLM Summary:** Meta, the tech giant, is backing away from renewable energy efforts by leaving a major industry group focused on clean energy. This move comes as the company continues to invest in natural gas infrastructure, a shift that's sparking controversy among environmentalists. The decision highlights a potential disconnect between Meta's public sustainability goals and its current actions.

Meta has made significant investments in natural gas over the past year. Now it's dropping out of an industry renewable energy group.
