# Mobileye CEO Amnon Shashua to step aside as company pushes into robotaxis, robotics

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 22:40:08 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/mobileye-ceo-amnon-shashua-to-step-aside-as-company-pushes-into-robotaxis-robotics/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/mobileye-ceo-amnon-shashua-to-step-aside-as-company-pushes-into-robotaxis-robotics/)

**LLM Summary:** Mobileye CEO Amnon Shashua will be stepping down from his role, but will remain involved with the company as chairman of the board. This change comes as Mobileye continues to focus on developing autonomous driving technologies, including robotaxis and robotics. Shashua's transition will help the company move forward with its ambitious plans in these areas.

Shashua has been invited to take the chairman of the board seat.
