# AegisAI, founded by former Google security execs, lands $36M to stop AI-driven spear phishing

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 18:38:34 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/aegisai-founded-by-former-google-security-execs-lands-36m-to-stop-ai-driven-spear-phishing/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/aegisai-founded-by-former-google-security-execs-lands-36m-to-stop-ai-driven-spear-phishing/)

**LLM Summary:** AegisAI, a cybersecurity startup co-founded by former Google security executives, has secured $36 million in funding to combat AI-driven spear phishing attacks. The company's AI-powered technology analyzes emails like a human would, catching subtle anomalies that traditional security checks often miss. This approach aims to provide more effective protection against sophisticated phishing threats.

AegisAI co-founders developed AI agents that quickly analyze each message as a human would, paying attention to small anomalies that even the most elaborate checklist wouldn’t catch.
