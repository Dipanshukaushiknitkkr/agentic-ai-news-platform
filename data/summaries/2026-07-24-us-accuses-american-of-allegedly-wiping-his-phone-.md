# US accuses American of allegedly wiping his phone using a ‘duress’ password during border search

**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 17:53:30 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/us-accuses-american-of-allegedly-wiping-his-phone-using-a-duress-password-during-border-search/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/us-accuses-american-of-allegedly-wiping-his-phone-using-a-duress-password-during-border-search/)

**LLM Summary:** A US citizen is facing a court case after allegedly entering a 'duress' password on his phone during a border search, which wiped the device's data. This incident raises questions about an individual's rights when it comes to protecting their personal data at US borders. The citizen is now asking a court to dismiss the government's claim, arguing that their rights were violated.

A U.S. citizen has asked a court to throw out the government's claim that he gave over a passcode to border authorities that wiped his phone's data, opening up fresh questions about a person's constitutional rights at the U.S. border.
