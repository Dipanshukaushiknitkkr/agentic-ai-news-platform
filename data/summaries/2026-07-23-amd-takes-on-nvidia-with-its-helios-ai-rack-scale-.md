# AMD takes on Nvidia with its Helios AI rack-scale system

**Published:** Thu, 23 Jul 2026 20:33:02 +0000

**Link:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/amd-takes-on-nvidia-with-its-helios-ai-rack-scale-system/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/amd-takes-on-nvidia-with-its-helios-ai-rack-scale-system/)

**LLM Summary:** AMD is launching the Helios AI rack-scale system, a powerful computing platform designed to compete with NVIDIA's offerings in the field of artificial intelligence. This system will start shipping to customers later this year, marking a significant push by AMD into the AI market. With Helios, AMD aims to provide a high-performance alternative to NVIDIA's solutions for AI workloads.
